Agree on the cue. Keep both roles in view.

The Shared Scene Notebook keeps your own source notes, intentions and rehearsal observations together. Plan the shared scene while leaving room for each contribution.

Name the scene and source

Begin with a scene label and a source or rights note. Add each role’s immediate intention in your own words. Your notes do not establish permission or examination suitability.

Explore a response together

Record an agreed cue and responding role, the shared space and audience view, and a voice or phrasing experiment you want to explore.

Reflect on both contributions

Keep what each learner and listener noticed, a reason for a choice, and one next rehearsal test. These are your observations, not an automatic assessment.

Knowledge practice

Practise the knowledge behind the scene.

Review original questions with answer explanations on character intentions, listening and response, cues, voice, movement and reflection. Save questions to revisit and explore concept lessons.

This is an independent knowledge study aid. It does not administer the qualification, grade acting or approve your repertoire.

Scene time and reflection time belong apart.

Enter rehearsal minutes for the shared duologue and reflection separately. These are manual notes, not a running timer, a performance recording or a measured assessment.

Before your next rehearsal

Is this an official Trinity College London app?

No. Pair Acting Grade 1 Study is independently developed by RushLabs. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Trinity College London or any government body. It does not administer or grade the qualification. Consult the provider and your teacher for current requirements.

Does it provide an approved duologue?

No. Original knowledge practice does not establish examination repertoire approval. The notebook is for your own short scene, source and rehearsal notes; it does not supply official examination scripts, complete plays or performance recordings.

Are notes automatically shared with my partner?

No. Save keeps notes on this device, and reopening the notebook restores the saved notes. There is no partner messaging, account or cloud synchronisation. Keep separate copies of anything important.

Can I download the app now?

Not yet. A paid download is planned with the included study material and notebook. No separate subscription is enabled. Final price and regional availability will appear in the store when the app is released.
